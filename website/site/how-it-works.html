How it works

Four quiet steps.
Nothing leaves your Mac.

Save a file and Filo does the rest — reading, naming, and filing it, all on device. You stay in charge of every move.

01

It watches your Downloads

The moment a file lands, Filo notices. Nothing else on your Mac is watched or touched.

02

It reads the file, on device

The text in a PDF, the words in a screenshot — understood by the AI built into macOS. No upload, no network.

03

It suggests a name and a folder

A readable filename and the best match among the folders you've chosen — shown in a small, glanceable popup.

04

You decide

Accept, edit, or dismiss. Files move only when you accept — and when Filo launches, every move can be undone.

Getting set up

Three things, once.

01

Run macOS

Filo uses the on-device model that ships inside macOS.

02

Turn on Apple Intelligence

Enable it in System Settings. Without it, Filo still runs and handles names by type.

03

Choose your folders

Pick the folders Filo may suggest as destinations. It only ever suggests — nothing moves on its own.

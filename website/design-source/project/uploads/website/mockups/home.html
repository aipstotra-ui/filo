How it works

Four quiet steps — and nothing leaves your Mac.

01

It watches your Downloads

The instant a file arrives, the app notices. Nothing else on your Mac is watched or touched.

02

It reads the file, on device

The text of a PDF, the words in a screenshot — understood by the AI built into macOS. No upload, no network.

03

It suggests a name and a folder

A readable filename and the best match among the folders you’ve chosen, in a small glanceable popup.

04

You decide

Accept, edit, or dismiss. Files move only when you accept — and every move can be undone.

Nothing leaves
your Mac. Ever.

The AI ships inside macOS, so there is nothing to upload and nothing to download. Your files — and everything in them — stay yours.

Zero network calls

The app makes no network requests at all. This is enforced by code review, not just intended.

On-device AI

Everything is understood by the model built into macOS. No server ever sees your files.

You stay in control

Files are only ever renamed or moved after you accept — and every move can be undone.

What the app reads

Only enough to help — and only on your Mac.

To learn what a folder holds, the app reads its file names and briefly looks inside a few files. Nothing leaves your Mac.

A small text sample, never the whole file

To match a destination, Filo reads file names plus a short on-device text sample from about three files per folder. That sample is used to understand, then discarded — it is never stored or sent.

Files move only when you accept

Filo only ever suggests. A file is renamed or moved solely after you accept — and every move is logged and can be undone.

The waitlist

The one place we ask for anything.

The app itself never touches the network. This website's waitlist is the single exception: if you enter your email, it is used only to notify you when Filo launches. No spam, no sharing, no other use.

We intend to use a privacy-respecting email tool for this, chosen to stay on-brand with the product. Until then, the signup on this site is a preview and stores nothing.
